# NOTICE

## MedNourish — Educational Prototype

MedNourish is an educational software prototype and computer science research demonstration exploring nutritional deficiency reference matching, dietary habit planning, and transfer learning.

### Important Disclaimers

1. **Not a Medical Device**: MedNourish is NOT a medical device, clinical diagnostic tool, or clinical decision support system. It does not diagnose diseases, prescribe medications or dietary supplements, or replace consultation with a qualified physician or healthcare professional.
2. **Datasets and Model Weights Not Included**: In accordance with project governance rules, raw image and tabular training datasets (~31 GB, 394,000+ files) and trained neural network model binary weights (`*.keras`, `*.tflite`) are not distributed in this repository.
3. **Third-Party Data Provenance & Licences**: Third-party datasets investigated during academic evaluation (including public image corpora from Kaggle, DermNet, and academic publications) have unverified redistribution rights and provenance; they are retained solely in local private archives.
